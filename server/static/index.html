Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about SAHAYAK

Simply start a voice session and say "Meri complaint ka status check karo" or "Check my ticket status" and mention your ticket number (e.g., SHK-CIVIC-1234). SAHAYAK will instantly look up the current status and explain it in plain language. You can also find your ticket number in the Call Storage tab under the recording of the session when you filed the complaint.

Go to the 📁 Call Storage tab (next to the Conversation tab in the workspace below). All your past voice sessions are saved there automatically with a timestamp, duration, and transcript. You can search by keyword, date, or ticket number, and play back any recording directly in the browser. No login is required — recordings are stored locally on the server.

Yes! Each complaint session issues you a unique SAHAYAK Citizen PIN. If you lose access to your device, you can recover your PIN and recording history by visiting your nearest Municipal Ward Office or Jan Seva Kendra with a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter Card, etc.). The on-duty officer can look up your PIN using your mobile number and re-issue it to you. Bring the ticket number (e.g., SHK-CIVIC-XXXX) if you remember it — it speeds up the process significantly.

Absolutely. SAHAYAK understands all three naturally — you don't need to switch any setting. You can mix languages mid-sentence (e.g., "Bhai, mere gali mein 3 days se light nahi hai") and SAHAYAK will understand and respond. The AI automatically detects your language and replies in the same language you're using.

SAHAYAK immediately activates its Emergency Protocol. It will advise you to stay safe, keep a distance from the hazard, and not touch anything. The incident is escalated directly to the 112 Emergency Helpline. A red safety banner also appears on your screen with real-time safety instructions. Please also call 112 yourself if the situation is life-threatening — SAHAYAK is a reporting aid, not a replacement for emergency services.

Resolution time depends on the department and complaint type. Typical timelines are: Waste & Sanitation — 24–48 hours; Water Supply — 48–72 hours; Street Lighting — 3–5 working days; Potholes / Roads — 7–15 working days. You can always ask SAHAYAK to check your ticket status for real-time updates.

Yes. A family member, neighbour, or local shop owner can use SAHAYAK on your behalf. Simply ask them to start a voice session and mention the complainant's address and contact number clearly during the call. The generated ticket (SHK-CIVIC-XXXX) and PIN will be provided at the end of the session — note it down safely. Alternatively, walk into your nearest Jan Seva Kendra or Gram Panchayat office where a designated SAHAYAK kiosk can file the complaint for you.
