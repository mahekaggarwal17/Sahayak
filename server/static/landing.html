01 · Engine

Four-layer voice intelligence stack.

Every spoken word passes through a purpose-built pipeline: multilingual transcription, voice activity detection, real-time captioning and a civic domain classifier that routes each complaint to the right department.

01
Speech Transcription

Deepgram Nova-3 multilingual ASR converts Hindi, English and Hinglish speech in under 400 ms, preserving code-switches mid-sentence.

02
Voice Activity Detection

A frame-by-frame energy-and-ZCR detector isolates speech from street noise, rain and ambient city audio before the packet leaves the device.

03
Live Captions

Every word SAHAYAK speaks is streamed as a real-time caption over Agora RTM 2.x, so citizens on slow networks always read what they heard.

GPT-4o classifies every complaint into one of six civic domains, assigns a ticket number and selects the correct municipal department in one pass.

04 · Questions

Common questions.

Everything you need to know about how SAHAYAK works, what it handles and how recorded calls are stored.

Which civic issues can SAHAYAK handle?

SAHAYAK covers six municipal domains: Waste and Sanitation, Street Lighting and Electrical, Roads and Potholes, Water Supply and Connections, Ticket Registration and Tracking, and Urgent Public Safety Hazards (live wires, gas leaks, open manholes). Each maps to a real civic department and generates a reference ticket number.

Do I need to choose a language before calling?

No. SAHAYAK recognises Hindi, English and Hinglish mid-sentence without any mode switch. You can say "Bhai, street light on our road is kharab ho gayi hai" and it will parse, classify and respond naturally in the same register you used.

How are recorded calls stored and who can access them?

Every session is recorded client-side via the Web Audio API, mixed from both mic and agent streams, and uploaded to the FastAPI backend as WebM Opus audio on call end. Recordings are stored server-side by session ID and are retrievable only via the same web client. No data leaves the server without an explicit download action.

What happens during an emergency like a live wire or gas leak?

The civic intent router flags Urgent Public Safety issues immediately. SAHAYAK interrupts its normal flow, issues clear safety instructions, and provides the national emergency helpline 112 verbally and as an on-screen alert banner.

Can I use SAHAYAK on a mobile device?

Yes. The web client is fully responsive and works in Chrome for Android and Safari for iOS. A native Android application built with Jetpack Compose is also included in the project, using the same Agora RTC/RTM SDK under the hood.

Is there a live transcript of what SAHAYAK says?

Yes. Agora RTM 2.x streams a per-turn transcription payload carrying the text and turn status. The web client renders it in a live captions card that updates word-by-word as the agent speaks, and the full transcript is saved alongside the audio at call end.
